Speedy Box – Privacy Policy

Developer: Giannis Jome Games · Last updated: 22 November 2026
ENGLISH

This policy explains what information the mobile game Speedy Box (the “Game”) uses and why. The Game is made by Giannis Jome Games (“we”).

In short: we do not ask for your name, email or any account. Your game progress is saved only on your phone. The Game shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google may use your device’s advertising ID and similar data to show and measure ads, based on the choices you make in the consent message.

1. Information we do not collect

We do not collect, store or sell personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. The Game has no sign-in and no chat.

2. Information stored on your device

Your progress (coins, stars, unlocked levels and vehicles, settings) is saved in a file on your own device. It never leaves your device and we cannot see it. Uninstalling the Game deletes it.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob: optional “watch an ad” videos that give a reward, and occasional full-screen ads between levels. To show, limit and measure these ads, Google may collect and process:

  • your device’s advertising ID (Android Advertising ID),
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version),
  • ad interactions (ads seen or tapped) and diagnostic data.

Google acts as an independent controller of this data. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): when you first open the Game you see Google’s consent message, where you can accept or refuse personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time from Settings → Privacy in the Game.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone’s settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • If you refuse personalised ads, you may still see non-personalised ads.

4. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, contact us and we will help remove it.

5. Data security and retention

We do not hold any personal data ourselves. Data processed by Google for advertising is kept according to Google’s own policies.

6. Your rights (GDPR)

If you are in the European Union you have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data, and to complain to your data protection authority (in Greece: the Hellenic Data Protection Authority, www.dpa.gr). Because we do not store personal data, requests about advertising data are best made to Google; we are happy to help.

7. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.

8. Contact

Questions about privacy: giannisjome@hotmail.com
